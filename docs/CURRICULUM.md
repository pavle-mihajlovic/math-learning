# Curriculum

**Strict order, top to bottom.** One unit = one book section. Every section and every exercise in the book is covered.
Scope for now: **Stage 1 only** (calculus). Stages 2–7 are tentative, to be planned later.

Each unit is turned into app content by one chat (task `K-<unit id>`, see ROADMAP), and the app splits it into equal two-minute chunks.

Every unit gets:
1. A **required reading**: an explanation adapted from the textbook section, which you page through before problems unlock.
2. **Optional proof cards** for the theorems in that section.
3. **All of the book's end-of-section exercises**, plus new-number variants of anything you miss, in a separate "Practice mistakes" mode.

Solutions are labeled in the app:
- 📗 **book**: an answer written by the book's authors (odd-numbered exercises).
- ✅ **AI, verified by math software (SymPy)**.
- 🤖 **AI**.

Every unit with full worked solutions also gets the book's own worked examples.

| Stage | Book | Units | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OpenStax *Calculus* Vol 1–3 (Calc I–III, starts with a precalculus review) | 110 sections, about 6,300 exercises | Free CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 source files ([openstax/osbooks-calculus-bundle](https://github.com/openstax/osbooks-calculus-bundle)), no PDF needed |
| 2 | Linear algebra: Hefferon *Linear Algebra* | about 30 sections (mapped in task U-LA) | Free, full answer manual |
| 3 | Proofs: Hammack *Book of Proof* | about 30 sections (U-BOP) | Free, odd solutions |
| 4 | **Spivak *Calculus***, every chapter | 30 chapters (below) | Your copy (PDF or photos) + Spivak's *Answer Book* |
| 5 | Abbott *Understanding Analysis* (you own it) | about 40 sections (U-ABB) | Your copy |
| 6 | Rudin *Principles of Mathematical Analysis* | 11 chapters (U-PMA) | Your copy + community solutions |
| 7 | Rudin *Real and Complex Analysis* | 20 chapters (U-RCA) | Your copy |

Repeated chapters are skipped: Vol 2 ch. 1–2 = Vol 1 ch. 5–6, and Vol 3 ch. 1 = Vol 2 ch. 7.
Exercise counts are end-of-section exercises only. The worked examples and checkpoint problems inside each section are extra.
