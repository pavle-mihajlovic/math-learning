# Questions to lock down before building

Answer inline (short answers are fine, e.g. `A3: iPhone, Mac`).

## A. You and your starting point
1. What did you take before (Calc I/II/III, LA, any proof course), and how long ago?
2. How rusty are you? Could you do a u-substitution or row reduce a 3×3 matrix right now?
3. Have you written proofs before (induction, ε-δ)?
4. Is the goal understanding for its own sake, grad school, quant/ML work, or something else? It changes how deep each phase goes.
5. Realistic time per week? How much of it is 1–2 min phone bursts and how much is longer sit-down sessions?
6. Target timeline to start Baby Rudin? To finish Big Rudin?

## B. Devices and sync
7. Phone: iPhone or Android? Computer: Mac, Windows or Linux? Any tablet with a stylus (iPad + Pencil)?
8. OK with a web app installed to your home screen (no App Store), or do you want a native app?
9. Must it work **offline** (subway, plane)?
10. Sign in once with email magic link, or Google/Apple login?

## C. Books and PDFs
11. Which PDFs do you already have? List them, including **solution manuals** (e.g. Stewart Student Solutions Manual, Spivak Answer Book).
12. Open to free open-licensed books (OpenStax, Hefferon, Hammack, Axler 4e)? They give the cleanest pipeline.
13. Are your PDFs **text-based or scanned**? Try selecting text in one.
14. Which edition of Stewart (or another calculus book) do you want to anchor on?
15. Spivak or Abbott as the bridge into Rudin? Or straight to Rudin with lots of support?
16. For Rudin, do you have any solution sets, or should solutions come from AI critique plus community sources?

## D. Practice style
17. On the phone, do you mostly want to **(a)** work on paper and tap reveal, **(b)** type answers for auto-checking, or **(c)** both?
18. Is a math keyboard for typing `\int x e^x dx`-type answers acceptable, or too clunky on a phone?
19. Would you photograph handwritten work for AI feedback, or is that too much friction?
20. Hints before the solution (nudge → key idea → full solution), or straight to the solution?
21. How do you feel about **AI-generated** solutions when the book has none? Show them labeled, or only book and CAS-verified ones?
22. Want flashcards for definitions and theorems mixed into the same queue?
23. How many problems per unit is "a shitton": 30? 100? Every problem in the book?
24. Strict order (unit by unit) or interleaved (mixing old units in)? Interleaving works better for retention but feels harder.
25. Want a placement test per unit to skip what you already know?

## E. Motivation and UX
26. Streaks, daily goals, stats? Or minimal and quiet?
27. Daily reminder notifications (push to phone)?
28. Dark mode, font size: any accessibility needs?
29. Show the original PDF page crop next to the extracted problem, for trust and figures?

## F. Build and ops
30. Will you build alongside me (what languages do you know?), or should I build and you just use it?
31. Budget for the Claude API (extraction plus tutoring) and hosting? Is $0 hosting a must?
32. Fine with Supabase and Vercel (free tiers), or do you prefer self-hosting or Firebase?
33. Keep this GitHub repo **private**? Recommended, since extracted problems from commercial books shouldn't be public.
34. Where will the PDFs live: Google Drive, on your computer, or uploaded somewhere? (The ingestion step needs to read them.)
35. Would an **Anki-export-only** v0 (almost no building) be acceptable as a stopgap while the real app is built?
